Good to know

  • It works best on one instrument or voice at a time. Full band mixes come out busy: every instrument, including the drums' pitched ring, turns into notes.
  • Everything happens locally. The first run downloads a 1 MB model; long files take a while on slow machines (roughly real time or faster on a laptop with a GPU).
  • Want sheet music instead? The sheetmusic command-line tool in this repository writes MuseScore scores with key, metre and hands.
